    Office Bug Hunt

    The release is tomorrow. The devs are squashing bugs. Somebody in this office is quietly putting them back.

    How to play
    • Developers walk to their ticket desks (yellow) and fix tiny coding bugs. Fix every ticket in the sprint, or let go of every spy, to win.
    • Spies blend in, take coworkers offline, push bad commits that reopen fixed tickets, and can take production down. Spies win when they match the devs in number, or prod stays down.
    • Find a logged-off coworker? Report it to call a standup. Or ring the Standup Bell in the Conference Room (once per game).
    • At standup, read the chat, then vote someone out or skip.
    • Taken offline? You become a ghost: you can still fix tickets.
